FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does a roof installation take?

The duration of a roof installation depends on various factors such as the size of the roof, weather conditions, and the complexity of the project. Our team will provide you with a detailed timeline during the initial consultation.

What type of warranties do you offer ?

We offer both workmanship and manufacturer warranties to ensure long-lasting protection for your roof. Warranty coverage depends on the materials and services selected.

Do you provide free estimates ?

Yes, we provide free roofing estimates for all inspections, repairs, and new installations. Our team will evaluate your roof and give you a clear, detailed, no-obligation quote. This helps you understand the cost before starting any work.

What payment options do you accept ?

We accept multiple payment options including cash, credit/debit cards, and bank transfers. Flexible payment plans may also be available for larger roofing projects. We'll discuss all options clearly before starting the work.

Are you licensed and insured ?

Yes, we are fully licensed and insured to carry out roofing work. This ensures your property and our team are protected during every project. We always follow local safety and building regulations.

Should I replace my roof if it's not leaking ?

If your roof is not leaking, replacement is usually not immediately necessary. However, age, wear, or damaged shingles may still indicate future issues. A professional inspection can help decide if repairs or replacement is better.
